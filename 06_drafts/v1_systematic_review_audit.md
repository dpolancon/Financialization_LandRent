## Skill rules applied

- Reconstructed the HST literature on its own terms before critique.
- Organized the review by mechanisms rather than by paper summaries.
- Preserved the five analytical dimensions: definitions, mechanisms, causality, variables/parameters, and theory/evidence.
- Used author-year citations to original papers, not NotebookLM notes.
- Avoided invented page numbers.
- Marked unresolved gaps with `[citation detail needed]` or `[source gap]`.
- Preserved anti-conflation rules: land rent is not housing rent; land price is not real-estate price; wealth is not productive capital; credit expansion is not automatically productive investment; bubble is not irrationality.

## Memo claims expanded

- Land-price inflation can increase wealth without increasing productive capacity.
- Credit must be analyzed by sectoral allocation, not aggregate expansion alone.
- OLG in SHT solves a formal asset-circulation problem and need not be adopted as the project's ontology.
- SHT unbalanced growth is a formal productivity/substitution condition and must not be collapsed into the project's broader concept of disproportional growth.
- Land overvaluation connects land prices, rents, store-of-value demand, and growth.
- R versus G and debt rollover are connected to land through formal OLG results, but translation to private balance sheets is a project extension.
