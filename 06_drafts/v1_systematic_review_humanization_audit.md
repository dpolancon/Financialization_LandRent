## Humanize-Writing Overrides Applied

- Conversational advice, contractions, direct reader address, humor, and blog/marketing guidance were rejected as inappropriate for formal scholarly prose.
- Blacklist pressure was not applied to protected technical terms such as leverage, collateral, mechanism, synthesis, systematic review, unbalanced growth, land overvaluation, productive capital, land rent, real estate, wealth, bubble, R versus G, debt rollover, Pareto efficiency, and dynamic inefficiency.
- Academic/technical guidance was applied selectively: remove empty scaffolding, vary sentence rhythm, strengthen causal verbs, avoid generic evaluators, and keep technical terms precise.
