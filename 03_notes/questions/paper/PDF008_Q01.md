## NotebookLM question

What is the positive feedback loop between capital investment and land price in the model?

## NotebookLM answer

> [NOTEBOOKLM] In this model, the positive feedback loop between capital investment and land prices operates through a demand-driven financial accelerator fueled by aggregate wealth dynamics. An initial increase in the land price directly raises current aggregate wealth [1, 2]. This higher wealth expands the equity base of productive agents, allowing them to borrow against their leverage constraint and make larger capital investments, which in turn increases the next period's aggregate capital, aggregate output, and future wealth [1, 2]. The expansion in aggregate capital mechanically increases the marginal product of land (land rents), which further boosts the fundamental and speculative demand for land as a store of value [1]. When financial leverage exceeds a critical threshold, this self-reinforcing interaction becomes so strong that the macro-economy loses its balanced-growth property and takes off into endogenous unbalanced growth, causing land prices to grow faster than rents and generating a rational asset price bubble [3, 4].

## Distilled note

- **Core claim**: The model features a powerful financial accelerator where rising land prices increase aggregate wealth, fueling greater capital investment that recursively drives up land rents and asset demand, ultimately triggering unbalanced endogenous growth and land bubbles when leverage is sufficiently high.
- **Mechanism**:
  - An increase in the current land price directly raises current aggregate wealth.
  - Higher aggregate wealth expands the net worth (equity) of productive agents, allowing them to leverage up and significantly increase capital investment.
  - This elevated investment generates greater future capital, which raises both future overall wealth and land rents (the marginal product of land).
  - The resulting increase in future wealth and rents feeds back into even higher demand for land as a store of value, driving the current land price up further.
- **Formal object/result, if any**: The aggregate wealth equation $W_t = F(K_t, 1) + P_t$ (3.3), the capital investment equation $K_{t+1} = \beta\lambda W_t \int_{\overline{z}_t}^{\infty} z d\Phi(z)$ (3.4), and the land price equation $P_t = \beta W_t (\lambda\Phi(\overline{z}_t) + 1 - \lambda)$ (3.5).
- **Relevance for the project memo**: land-price inflation, land overvaluation
- **Critical caveat**: Evaluating this positive feedback loop for Latin America requires external verification, as the model's financial accelerator relies on uniformly distributed leverage limits and domestic wealth accumulation, abstracting from the region's exposure to volatile foreign capital inflows, dollarized real estate markets, and structural inequalities in land ownership.
