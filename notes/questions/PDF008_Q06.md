## NotebookLM answer

> [NOTEBOOKLM] The provided source does not explicitly mention or contrast its framework with the "OLG Hirano-Stiglitz wobbly-dynamics papers," meaning a direct comparison requires external verification. However, the text does explicitly distinguish its overall approach from earlier overlapping generations (OLG) bubble models (such as Wilson 1981 and Tirole 1985) [1]. Instead of using an OLG framework, this paper constructs a "standard macro-finance model with infinitely-lived heterogeneous agents" [1] who face idiosyncratic productivity shocks and leverage constraints [2, 3]. Consequently, rather than generating instability through the life-cycle savings behavior of finite-lived agents, this model generates unbalanced bubbly growth strictly through a financial accelerator tied to the borrowing limits (leverage) of infinitely-lived productive entrepreneurs [2].
