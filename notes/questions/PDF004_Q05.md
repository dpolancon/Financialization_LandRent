The text formally links its sections to earlier papers: Part I on wobbly dynamics is based on Hirano and Stiglitz (2022a) [2]; Part II on wobbly economies with land is based on Hirano and Stiglitz (2022b) [3]; and Part IV on endogenous growth with land and credit frictions is a variant of the model proposed in Hirano and Stiglitz (2024) [4]. - **Mechanism**:

  - The paper's contribution is unifying these prior insights to demonstrate that tractable, global OLG models can effectively replace standard dynastic models for analyzing macroeconomic instability.
